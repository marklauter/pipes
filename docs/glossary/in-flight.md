The state of a digital artifact between its arrival at an in-side door and its last departure.

## Examples

- An email attachment that has been extracted but not yet written to its destination folder.

## Rationale

"Belt" lost. It named a mechanism in the warehouse, and the domain needs only the state of the digital artifact between doors. The plugin pipeline acts on in-flight digital artifacts.

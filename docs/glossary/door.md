A delivery mechanism for passing digital artifacts between pipes and an external system.

## Examples

- The `scans` folder.
- The `invoices` mailbox.
- A partner's SFTP server.

## Rationale

The term comes from warehouse loading doors. "Dock" and "connection" lost. A folder counts, because digital artifacts can be dropped into it.

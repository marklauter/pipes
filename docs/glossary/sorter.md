A part of pipes for diverting in-flight digital artifacts to out-side doors by a sort plan.

## Rationale

The term comes from warehouse sortation. The sorter is the actor and the sort plan is the plan it applies. Sending a digital artifact to a failure door is also a diversion. "Kicker" lost.

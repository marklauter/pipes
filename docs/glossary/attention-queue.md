An out-side door for digital artifacts awaiting human attention.

## Examples

- A `review` folder, where a person checks digital artifacts that no route names.
- An issue-tracker queue, where each failed departure opens a ticket.

## Rationale

The warehouse equivalent is the exceptions area. A route can name it, and it is the fallback when no route applies or a departure fails. A released digital artifact arrives again through an in-side door.

A part of pipes for diverting in-flight digital artifacts to out-side doors by a sort plan.

## Rationale

The term comes from warehouse sortation. The sorter is the actor and the sort plan the data it applies. Sending a digital artifact with no route, or a failed departure, to the [[attention-queue]] is also a diversion. "Kicker" lost.
